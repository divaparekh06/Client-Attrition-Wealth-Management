# Paper Summaries

Each paper I reverse-engineer for this project gets a summary file here,
written in my own words **before** I check my understanding against AI or
anyone else. This is the reading step of the project — the goal is genuine
understanding, not a literature review for its own sake.

## Shortlisted papers/sources

1. Investigating customer churn in banking: a machine learning approach and
   visualization app for data science and management (ScienceDirect, 2023)
   — methodological backbone.
2. Forecasting client retention — A machine-learning approach (ScienceDirect)
   — retention framing + business-facing visualization.
3. Reducing Customer Attrition with Machine Learning for Financial
   Institutions (SAS practitioner paper) — template for risk × value ranking.
4. Offsetting Client Attrition: A Playbook for Wealth Managers (Alpha FMC)
   — industry framing, wealth-management-specific vocabulary and figures.

## Summary template

Use one file per paper: `01-short-title.md`, `02-short-title.md`, etc.

```markdown
# [Paper title]

**Source:** (link)
**Read on:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Problem they addressed
(In your own words — what real-world problem motivated this paper?)

## Method / approach
(What technique(s) did they use? Why, according to them?)

## What they measured success by
(Which metric(s)? Why did they choose those over alternatives?)

## What I'd borrow for my project
(Which parts of their approach are directly usable?)

## Limitations I noticed
(Be critical — what would you question or do differently, and why?)

## Open questions
(Anything you didn't fully understand — to research or ask about.)
```
